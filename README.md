### 🧠 What I do

- 🔧 **Embedded & firmware in C** — custom [Flipper Zero](https://flipperzero.one/) firmware: Sub-GHz/RF, NFC/RFID, automotive rolling-code work, and a BLE App Bridge protocol I designed from scratch.
- 📱 **Native Apple platforms** — SwiftUI apps for iOS & macOS: BLE companions, menu-bar utilities, and low-level system tooling.
- 📈 **Fintech & quantitative tooling in Python** — scenario simulators for the Russian market (IMOEX / MOEX, key rate, FX, commodities) with Monte Carlo and sensitivity analysis.
- 🧩 I like the full vertical: **firmware → transport (BLE) → companion app → analytics.**
